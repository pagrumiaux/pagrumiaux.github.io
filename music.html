Background

My Musical Journey

Music has shaped who I am long before I ever wrote a line of code. Starting piano lessons at age 6, I quickly developed a deep connection with the instrument. Beyond the classical training, I was drawn to understanding how music works — which naturally led me to playing by ear, improvising, and eventually arranging and composing.

In my teenage years, I discovered electronic music production, which opened a new world of sonic possibilities. I started experimenting with DAWs (Ableton Live), synthesizers, and sound design. This technical curiosity about sound eventually merged with my academic path, leading me to specialize in audio signal processing and machine learning for music.

Today, my musical practice and my engineering work inform each other. Understanding music from the inside — as a performer and composer — gives me intuition that pure data scientists often lack when working on audio AI. And conversely, my technical knowledge helps me approach composition with a unique perspective on timbre, spectrum, and acoustic space.
