Research

My academic work focuses on deep learning for audio signal processing, with particular expertise in spatial audio analysis and speaker localization.

Research Interests

My research sits at the intersection of deep learning and audio signal processing. During my PhD, I developed neural network architectures for analyzing spatial audio signals, specifically for counting and localizing speakers in reverberant environments using Ambisonics recordings.

Beyond localization, I'm deeply interested in music information retrieval, audio synthesis, and generative models for audio. My postdoctoral research explored bandwidth extension of musical signals using differentiable signal processing techniques.

I believe the future of audio AI lies in models that understand sound the way musicians do — not just as spectrograms, but as meaningful acoustic events with temporal structure and context.
