About Me

Deep Learning Engineer building AI systems for audio. PhD in spatial audio processing, pianist for 25+ years. Based in France.

Hello!

I'm Pierre-Amaury, a Deep Learning Engineer specializing in audio AI. I build intelligent systems that understand sound — from research prototypes to production-ready models running on embedded devices.

My journey into audio AI started with music. I've been playing piano since I was 6, which led me to study acoustics, signal processing, and eventually machine learning applied to audio. This path took me through a PhD at Orange Labs and GIPSA-lab, where I developed deep learning methods for speaker localization using spatial audio.

Today, I work as Lead Deep Learning Engineer at Sonaid, a startup focused on audio event recognition. As the sole ML engineer, I've built the entire deep learning pipeline from scratch — from dataset creation and model architecture design to evaluation frameworks and embedded deployment. It's challenging work that requires both research creativity and engineering pragmatism.

Outside of work, I continue to compose music. My original piano pieces are available on streaming platforms, blending my classical training with contemporary influences. I believe that being a musician makes me a better audio engineer — I understand sound not just as data, but as something meaningful.
