Piano is very important in my life. I have been playing it almost daily, since the age of 6. Over the years I have explored classical repertoire, blues, boogie-woogie, and minimal music. I have learned how to play by ear while exploring arrangements and compositions.

Piano

I compose original piano pieces and arrangements influenced by artists like Doug Hammer, Ludovico Einaudi, Guillaume Poncelet, Olexandr Ignatov, Pieter Savenberg, as well as cinematic scores. My compositions blend minimalist patterns with melodic storytelling, exploring melancholic and contemplative atmospheres. I have had the chance to perform some of these pieces at friends' weddings and continue to share them on streaming platforms.
